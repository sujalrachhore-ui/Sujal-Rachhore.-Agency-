## 🚀 Key Modules & System Capabilities

### 1. Public Institute Website
- **Hero & Trust Metrics:** 5,000+ Students Mentored, 25+ Expert Educators, 92%+ Selection Rate, 10+ Years of Excellence.
- **Academic Programs Showcase:** Detailed breakdowns for JEE Main + Advanced, NEET Medical UG, MHT-CET Ranker, 11th & 12th Science Board, and Junior Foundation.
- **Proven Results & AIR Toppers:** Scorecards with filters by exam and year (AIR 84 JEE, AIR 42 NEET 705/720, MHT-CET 99.98%ile).
- **Faculty Council:** Academic qualifications, teaching tenures, and specializations.
- **4-Step Mastery Cycle:** Concept Inception, Hierarchical DPPs, Computer-Based Diagnostic CBT, and Micro-Analytics Mentorship.
- **Campus Gallery:** Interactive gallery with category filtering (Classrooms, Campus, Achievements, Seminars, Events).
- **Verified Testimonials:** Real parent, student, and alumnus feedback with 5-star ratings.
- **Live Circulars:** Official notice board with downloadable attachment links.
- **Campus Location & Inquiry:** Embedded Nagpur Google Map view, direct WhatsApp chat, and interactive admissions inquiry form.

### 2. Student Portal
- **Dashboard:** Attendance rate ring, pending tests counter, latest exam percentile, fee balance, and schedule summary.
- **Identity & Profile:** Verified admission number, course allocation, guardian details, and contact updater.
- **Attendance Calendar:** Monthly attendance percentage, present/absent logs, and late arrival flags.
- **Online MCQ Test Engine:** 
  - Real countdown timer in minutes and seconds with automatic submission on expiration.
  - Question palette navigation (Answered, Unanswered, Marked for Review).
  - Negative marking algorithm (-2 points for wrong answers).
  - Detailed diagnostic scorecard with question-by-question derivation review.
- **Study Materials & Digital Library:** Subject-wise categorization, PDF document viewer, download links, and video lecture embeds.
- **Fee Ledger & Payments:** Outstanding due balance tracker, receipt generation, and simulated multi-channel checkout (UPI, Net Banking, Card).
- **Batch Timetable:** Weekly schedule with real-time "Today" class indicator.
- **Helpdesk & Grievance Cell:** Direct academic inquiry ticket creation and direct WhatsApp counselor link.

### 3. Faculty / Teacher Portal
- **Batches & Roster:** View assigned student cohorts with contact details and guardian information.
- **Biometric Roll Call:** Interactive roll call grid (Present, Late, Absent) with 1-click "Mark All Present" shortcut and teacher remarks.
- **Test Bank Builder:** Create multi-question MCQ assessments, configure time limits, negative marking weights, and publishing status.
- **Study Material Publishing:** Distribute PDF summaries, formula sheets, and video lecture links to batches.
- **Student Performance Review:** Scorecard analysis with CSV export.

### 4. Administration Management Console
- **Executive KPI Dashboard:** Total enrollment, faculty council, fees collected, receivables due, and course distribution charts.
- **Student Admissions CRM:** Enroll new students, assign batch & admission ID, edit dossiers, or deactivate accounts.
- **Faculty Management:** Onboard teachers, configure experience years, assign subjects and batches.
- **Course & Batch Catalogs:** Manage course pricing, descriptions, classroom allocations, and capacity thresholds.
- **Fee Accounting & Cashier:** Record offline cashier entries, monitor student payment history, and generate vouchers.
- **Admission Leads CRM:** Pipeline management with lead stages (`NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `FOLLOW_UP`, `CONVERTED`, `CLOSED`).
- **Master Timetable Scheduler:** Weekly lecture room and faculty allocation.
- **System Settings:** Update institute address, phone, email, WhatsApp, and reset to initial seed demo data anytime.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide React Icons
- **State Management & Persistence:** Normalized relational state store with automatic `localStorage` synchronization and reset triggers
- **Design Philosophy:** EdTech SaaS aesthetic, rounded cards, subtle shadows, typography hierarchy, mobile-first responsive layout
- **Zero Broken Buttons:** Every interaction (taking tests, scoring tests, taking attendance, recording fee payments, submitting leads) is fully functional and immediately modifies application state.
